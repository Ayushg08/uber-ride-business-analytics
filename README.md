# 🚕 Uber Ride & Business Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Uber Ride & Business Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard turns Uber ride data into meaningful business insights by looking at bookings, revenue, customers, vehicle performance, cancellations, payment methods, trip distance, and locations.

The main goal of this project was to understand how different factors such as vehicle type, customer behavior, payment method, and location affect overall ride and business performance.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Understand overall Uber ride and booking performance
- Analyze revenue and trip distance
- Identify customer behavior and customer segments
- Compare performance across different vehicle types
- Analyze booking cancellations and their impact
- Understand customer payment preferences
- Identify popular pickup and drop-off locations
- Track important KPIs through an interactive dashboard

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 🏠 Overview

Provides a high-level view of the business with key metrics such as:

- Total Bookings
- Lost Bookings
- Total Revenue
- Total Distance
- Average Trip Distance
- Customer Cancellations
- Driver Cancellations
- Payment Methods
- Booking and revenue trends

### 🚗 Vehicle Analysis

Analyzes different vehicle types based on:

- Bookings
- Revenue
- Trip distance
- Customer ratings
- Driver ratings
- Vehicle performance

### 👥 Customer Analysis

Focuses on customer behavior, including:

- Customer type
- First-time customers
- Returning customers
- Regular customers
- Customer cancellations
- Booking patterns
- Revenue contribution

### 📍 Location Analysis

Explores:

- Pickup locations
- Drop-off locations
- Booking distribution by location
- Revenue by location
- Trip distance across locations

## 🔑 Key KPIs

Some of the major metrics included in the dashboard are:

- **93K+ Total Bookings**
- **57K+ Lost Bookings**
- **52M+ Total Revenue**
- **2.5M+ Total Distance**
- **24.64 Average Trip Distance**
- **11K+ Customer Cancellations**
